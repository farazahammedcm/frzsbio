Founder background

Faraz Ahammed CM is the CEO and founder of FRZ Generations and the founder of HF Media Malayalam, based in Kerala, India. He started FRZ Generations in 2025 after finding that many of the apps students use did not feel secure, and, with 30+ websites built so far, he now leads its tools, games, school software and public-service apps. He built the entire FRZ ecosystem through prompt engineering rather than hand-written code, leading every product from idea to launch. His professional profile is on LinkedIn, and the brand's official link is support-frz.netlify.app.

FRZ Generations: goal and mission

Good study tools should be free, secure and available on any phone. When I looked at the apps students use, many did not feel secure. So, using prompt engineering with Claude.ai, I started building my own, step by step and based on demand: 2 apps, then 4, 8, 10 and more. By the end of Class 10 I had 25+ apps and renamed the whole thing FRZ Generations, turning FRZ Notes into one of its branches. It is non-profit, free forever, and has no login wall. It is part of FRZ Project 2K30, a plan to ship 30+ free platforms by 2030.
