CMRHSS Chennamangallur School Review by a Former Student (Class 9)

By Faraz Ahammed CM · 6 November 2026

Verdict: I studied at CMRHSS, Chennamangallur, in Class 9. It gave me the confidence that I was not weak in academics. This is my personal opinion as a former student.

Teachers

The teachers were the most supportive I have met. Many teachers hold PhDs, and they noticed effort and pushed students forward.

Programs and ranking

The school runs many programs and is ranked among the highest schools in Kozhikode district. My marks went from 75% in the Onam exam to 90% in the Christmas exam (rank 6 in class, 23rd of 300 in school).

A competitive, motivating culture

The school has many features that give students a push and the mindset to compete and win. My class, in my view, was the highest-IQ class I have been part of.

Campus and surroundings

The school stands on top of a hill with a beautiful view. There are many shops nearby, and many of them allow credit for hostel students and KC students.

Community and reputation

The school is mainly Muslim-populated and highly recognised.

Who it suits

Students who want supportive teachers and a competitive, structured environment, in the Kozhikode and Malappuram area.
