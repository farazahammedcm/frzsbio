From Average Student to Founder: Faraz Ahammed CM's Story

By Faraz Ahammed CM · 6 November 2026

Short answer: Faraz Ahammed CM was an unnoticed average student up to Class 8. A hostel, a supportive school and some honest criticism changed that, and by the end of Class 10 he had scored 90% in SSLC and built 25+ free apps that became FRZ Generations.

Up to Class 8: unnoticed at KDUEMS Vazhakkad

I was a small, average child who mixed in and went unnoticed. In my last year I received a few participation and sports certificates, but I was still in the background. An uncle often said I would stay unnoticed and never rise above average marks.

Class 9: Islahiya Hostel and CMRHSS

I moved to Islahiya Hostel, Chennamangallur, and studied at CMRHSS. I did not have a special skill. What I needed was the right surroundings and situation, and I got them. In the Onam exam I scored 75%, outside the top 10. My parents offered a PC if I scored 90%. In the Christmas exam I scored 90%, rank 6 in class and 23rd in school out of 300. CMRHSS gave me the confidence that I was not weak in academics. The hostel gave me friends from all over Kerala and many good memories.

Class 10: building my own apps

I moved to PMSAPTHSS Kakkove for Class 10 (HS division); little changed there. I scored 90% in SSLC. Many study apps did not feel secure, so with Claude.ai I started building my own, step by step: 2 apps, then 4, 8, 10 and more. By the end of Class 10 I had 25+ apps, and I renamed FRZ Notes into a branch of a new brand, FRZ Generations. I also built games with AI, which became FRZ Games.

This chapter is still being lived, so its story is in the queue and will be published here soon.

Lesson: Criticism is information, and the right environment matters more than a special talent.
