FRZ Games: Free Browser Games Built with AI for Students

By Faraz Ahammed CM, CEO and founder of FRZ Generations · 9 November 2026

Short answer: FRZ Games is the games branch of FRZ Generations, available at frz-games.netlify.app. The games were built with the same AI-based method as the rest of the brand and are free to play in a browser.

Why games?

After the study tools were running, I wanted FRZ Generations to also give students a place to relax and compete. I built games using the same AI process and named the platform FRZ Games.

Free and instant

There is nothing to install, so the games run on a phone or a computer. Games share a leaderboard so players can compete.

Part of the brand

FRZ Games sits next to FRZ Public Library and the other branches. Read how it was built through prompting.
