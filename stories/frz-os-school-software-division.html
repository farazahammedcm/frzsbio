FRZ OS: The School Software Division of FRZ Generations

By Faraz Ahammed CM, CEO and founder of FRZ Generations · 9 November 2026

Short answer: FRZ OS is the operating-system and school-software division of FRZ Generations, at frzos.netlify.app. It holds school-focused software built by Faraz Ahammed CM.

Freezy OS (discontinued)

The first product in the division was Freezy OS, a browser-based desktop OS. I discontinued it because of privacy threats, because keeping users safe matters more than keeping a product alive.

SchoolSync

SchoolSync was an app idea for a free replacement for the private software that schools pay for. It laid the foundation for ONE H OS.

ONE H OS

ONE H OS (1HOS, the Plus One Humanities Operating System) is a class ERP made specially for a Humanities batch at PMSAPTHSS Kakkove.

FRZ EC

FRZ EC is a heavily protected election software developed for schools.

The idea

Schools should have secure, free, modern software that students can help shape. More about the brand: how FRZ Generations started. The full Class 11 Humanities story is in the queue.
