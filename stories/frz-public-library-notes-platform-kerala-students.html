FRZ Public Library: A Free Notes Platform for Kerala Students

By Faraz Ahammed CM, CEO and founder of FRZ Generations · 9 November 2026

Short answer: FRZ Public Library is a free notes platform for Kerala students at publiclibrary-frz.netlify.app. It grew out of FRZ Notes and covers SSLC and Plus One Humanities notes and papers in one place.

Where it came from

FRZ Notes started as my own notes, published online with more than 20 verified users. When it became a branch of FRZ Generations, it kept growing.

Why it became a library

When my Humanities notes for Class 11 started coming in, many of my Class 10 teachers asked me to share them. That request pushed me to merge everything into a single unified app, FRZ Public Library, holding Class 10 and Class 11 notes together.

What students get

Free access, no login wall, and one place to find notes and papers instead of searching many sites. It works in any browser on a phone.

Why it stays free

FRZ Generations is non-profit. The library follows the same promise as the rest of the brand: study material should not depend on money. Read how FRZ Generations started.
