How FRZ Generations Started: A Free, Non-Profit Ed-Tech Brand from Kerala

By Faraz Ahammed CM, CEO and founder of FRZ Generations · 9 November 2026

Short answer: FRZ Generations is a non-profit ed-tech brand founded in 2025 by Faraz Ahammed CM in Kerala, India. It began as a personal notes site called FRZ Notes and grew into 25+ free tools, games and school software for students.

The first step: my own notes

Good notes were scattered across many websites. I wrote my own on my laptop and published them online. I did not have many name options, so I used my own name, Faraz, as the brand. That became FRZ Notes, and it reached more than 20 verified users. It was the first version of FRZ Generations.

The problem: apps that did not feel secure

I then looked at the new apps students were using and found that many of them did not feel secure. Instead of accepting that, I checked whether I could make my own with the help of Claude.ai.

Growing step by step

I built 2 apps, then 4, then 8, then 10, and kept going based on demand. By the end of Class 10 I had more than 25 apps. I renamed the whole brand FRZ Generations and turned FRZ Notes into one of its branches.

What FRZ Generations stands for

It is non-profit and free forever, with no login wall. It is part of FRZ Project 2K30, a plan to ship 30+ free platforms by 2030. The official link is support-frz.netlify.app.

Where it went next

The brand grew into several branches: FRZ Public Library for notes, FRZ Games, FRZ OS for school software, and PDF Express. See how I built it all with prompt engineering.
