PDF Express: A Free, Open-Source PDF App Made for the Kerala Government

By Faraz Ahammed CM, CEO and founder of FRZ Generations · 9 November 2026

Short answer: PDF Express is a free, open-source PDF app under the MIT licence, built for the Kerala government and publicly available at pdfexpress-frz.netlify.app. Its price is zero rupees, whether or not the government accepts it.

Why I built it

After many requests from officials, and from my own experience, I made special tools for government offices.

The Excel to PDF tool

The first was a site that uses Excel equations to turn Word cells into a PDF, saving officials repeated manual work.

PDF Express

The newer app is PDF Express. It brings the common PDF jobs into one browser app: merge, split, organize pages, convert images to PDF, extract and view pages, read in a viewer, highlight, add text and redact.

What free and MIT-licensed means

Anyone can use, copy and improve it at no cost, and the code stays open. That is why it is safe to offer to the government at zero rupees.
